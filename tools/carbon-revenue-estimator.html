How this estimate works

We combine your land use type and current tillage practice (which set a baseline "headroom" for further improvement) with the specific regenerative practices you select, each of which has a published incremental carbon sequestration potential (tCO2e/acre/year). This gives an adjusted annual sequestration figure for your farm. We then apply three scenarios — conservative, base case, and optimistic — that vary both the crediting volume (after accounting for verification and buffer pool discounts) and the carbon price per tonne, to show a realistic revenue range rather than a single misleading number.
