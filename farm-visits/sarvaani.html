SARVAANI Farm

A working Karnataka coffee farm where five different systems run side by side — medicinal plants, native bees, vermicompost and biogas, livestock, and coffee. Kantharaju and his family teach all five, in the field, on the same day you visit.

What you will actually do

Five modules, taught in the field

Pick the ones you need. A half day usually covers two modules properly; a full day covers four to five with lunch in between. Tell us what you came to learn and Kantharaju builds the day around it.

1

Medicinal & Herbal Plants Walk

A guided sensory tour of the farm's medicinal plant life.

Key learnings
Identification, taste and human health benefits of native medicinal leaves — ಕಾಮ ಕಸ್ತೂರಿ Kaama Kasturi (sweet basil), lemon grass, Navrangi, mint and Kirataka.
Takeaway
How to use the plants already growing in your backyard as natural remedies for everyday health.
2

Apiculture (Beekeeping) Workshop

A deep dive into the life cycle of bees and the work they do for the farm around them.

Key learnings
Colony hierarchy and the roles inside a hive — worker bees, drones and the queen — with a special focus on native varieties including the stingless Mujanti bee and its life cycle.
Hands-on
Opening a box, reading a frame, and understanding what a healthy colony looks like before you keep one yourself.
3

Sustainable Energy & Composting

Circular farming and waste management, as actually practised on this farm.

Key learnings
The role of ಎರೆಹುಳು Erehula (earthworms) in turning farm waste into rich compost, and step-by-step guidance on building and running a gobar gas (biogas) plant — including the safety precautions people skip.
Takeaway
A realistic view of what a biogas unit costs, what it saves, and what it needs from you every week.
4

Livestock & Animal Husbandry

An introduction to integrated farm animal management.

Key learnings
Care requirements across the farm's livestock — the different cows, sheep and goats — and how they feed the compost, the biogas and the field.
5

Coffee Cultivation Tour

A backend look at what it takes to produce the perfect cup.

Key learnings
A walking tour through the coffee estate covering planting, cultivation and crop care through the season — and how the bees, shade trees and pepper vines fit into it.
