Verification

We reject most farms that apply.

A farm visit is only worth paying for if the farmer has something proven to teach and can keep you safe while teaching it. Five gates, scored out of 50. A listing needs 35 and zero red flags.

Proven agronomy · 15

Yields 25%+ above the local average, held for three years or more. Verified against sale receipts, mandi slips and a neighbour's word.

Teaching ability · 10

Scored on a mystery visit. Can the host explain why, admit what failed, and show real numbers — not just walk you around?

Guest safety · 10

Drinking water, clean toilet, shade, first-aid kit, no unfenced machinery, safe parking, phone reachable. Photographed and checked in person.

Hospitality · 10

Punctual, clean, and at least one person in the household fluent in your language. Food only from hosts who clear a kitchen check.

Documentation · 5

ID, land record, bank details and a signed code of conduct. Bonded labour, illegal borewells or caste-based exclusion mean instant rejection.

Re-certified every year. Rating floor of 4.2 across the last 10 visits — two bad visits and the listing is paused until we've re-visited and coached the host.

Questions

Before you book

How do I know the farm is worth the money?

Verified farms cleared the five-gate check described above, including a mystery visit where we paid as a guest and scored the day — the certification month is printed on the card. Farms still going through that process are labelled Pilot farm, and we tell you plainly which one you are booking. Either way: if a visit doesn't match its listing, tell us within 48 hours and we refund you.

What happens after I submit the request form?

You get a booking ID by email and WhatsApp immediately. We check the host's availability and confirm your date within 24 hours (usually within 6 working hours). Then you get a UPI payment link, and once paid, a ticket with the pin location, the host's number and your coordinator's number. Reminders go out three days and one day before.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Free reschedule up to 72 hours before the visit. Full refund if the host cancels or the visit is rained off. 50% refund between 24 and 72 hours, and no refund under 24 hours — by then the host has bought food and blocked the day.

Is this a training course or a farm holiday?

Both exist, and the card tells you which. Peer learning visits are for working farmers: crop calendar, technology demos, real cost sheets. Farm experience days are built for families and first-timers. Institutional programmes are curriculum-linked days for schools, colleges and CSR teams.

I learned something on the visit. Can I actually implement it on my land?

That's the point, and it's why Mujanti sits in the middle. What works on a 40-acre estate can bankrupt a two-acre holding. After a peer learning visit, our team adapts the practice to your land size, working capital, irrigation and labour, and sends a customised advisory — so you adopt a de-risked version, not a copy.

Are children allowed? Is it safe?

Yes, on farm experience days, accompanied by an adult. Every listed farm passed a physical safety check — drinking water, clean toilet, first aid, no unfenced machinery, controlled dogs. Group accident cover applies to every booked guest for the day of the visit.

I'm a farmer and I want to host. What's the catch?

No catch and no fee to apply. You keep 70%, paid within 72 hours. What we ask for is honesty about your numbers, a safe farm and punctuality. The bar is high because our reputation and yours are the same asset.

Booking for a group, a college, or a CSR team?

Cohorts of 15 to 60 get a structured curriculum, certificates, a Mujanti facilitator and an invoice for your finance team. Tell us the dates and the learning objective.
